
# Expense Tracker & Budget Manager

## 1. Problem Statement
Many individuals find it difficult to manage their income and expenses because of poor financial record-keeping. Manual tracking makes budgeting and monitoring spending difficult.

## 2. Project Objective
To develop a web-based application that helps users record income and expenses, manage monthly budgets, organize transactions, and monitor their financial activities.

## 3. Target Users
- Students
- Working Professionals
- Individuals managing personal finances

## 4. Core Modules
- User Management
- Category Management
- Expense Management
- Income Management
- Budget Management
- Reports and Dashboard

## 5. Project Scope
Included:
- User Authentication
- Income and Expense Management
- Category Management
- Monthly Budgets
- Transaction History
- Dashboard and Reports

Excluded:
- Live Bank Integration
- Online Payment Gateway
- Investment Management
- Cryptocurrency Tracking
- Tax Calculations
- Dedicated Mobile Application

## 6. Technology Stack
- MongoDB
- Express.js
- React.js
- Node.js

## 7. Expected Outcome
A secure and user-friendly application that helps users track financial activities, manage monthly budgets, reduce unnecessary spending, and make better financial decisions.
